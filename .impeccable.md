## Design Context

### Users

Potential employers, clients, and collaborators reviewing Kelvin Lamptey's technical experience, projects, and ways to get in touch.

### Brand Personality

Clean, confident, and technical. The portfolio should feel credible, direct, and professionally calm.

### Aesthetic Direction

Maintain the existing light, functional portfolio aesthetic: restrained blue accents, clear hierarchy, purposeful whitespace, and accessible interactions.

### Design Principles

- Make technical work easy to scan and verify.
- Prioritize clarity, credible claims, and direct paths to project sources.
- Use consistent typography, spacing, and interaction states.
- Keep pages responsive and keyboard accessible.
